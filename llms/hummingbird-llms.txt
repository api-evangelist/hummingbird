# Hummingbird

> Hummingbird is an AI-powered compliance operations platform for financial institutions and fintechs. It automates customer screening (sanctions, PEP, adverse media), transaction monitoring, investigations, and regulatory reporting (SAR/STR/CTR filing), orchestrating financial-crime and risk workflows across a connected app marketplace of 50+ integrations. Backed by Homebrew.

Note: Hummingbird does not currently publish a public, self-serve API specification or developer portal. Product APIs power custom two-way integrations and are provisioned through their partnerships/integrations team. This llms.txt is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from public company surfaces; there is no first-party OpenAPI to link.
